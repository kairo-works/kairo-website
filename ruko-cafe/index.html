Aussie brunch, Bali produce

Ruko is a small Australian-style cafe on Jalan Pantai Berawa, a short stroll from the beach. The food is fresh and healthy, made with locally sourced organic produce, and the coffee is Ruko’s own special Indonesian blend.

Come for a slow breakfast, a working lunch with good wifi, or a smoothie bowl after the surf. It is the kind of neighbourhood spot regulars come back to — some of them for years.
