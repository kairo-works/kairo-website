About

Bu means Mrs.
This is her warung.

Warung Bu Mi is a small Indonesian warung on Batu Bolong street — a nasi campur buffet: rice, and a counter of dishes cooked fresh each morning. No menu to study, no fuss.

It is the cheapest honest lunch in Canggu: walk up to the counter, point at what looks good, and sit down. Locals eat here, surfers eat here, and the plate costs about two dollars.

A few minutes’ walk from Batu Bolong beach, open every day from eight in the morning until ten at night.
