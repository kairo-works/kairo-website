Breakfast after a morning surf, a long lunch, then cocktails as the sun drops — the sun sets around 6pm all year in Bali, and La Rosa’s tables face straight at it. Batu Bolong is Canggu’s mellow, longboard-friendly surf beach, so the sand in front of the restaurant is busy from first light.

About

A beach table,
all day long

La Rosa is a beach-side restaurant and bar in the heart of Canggu, on Batu Bolong beach. It opens at 7 in the morning and does not close until midnight — breakfast after the surf, lunch in the shade, dinner and drinks as the sky turns orange.

The menu leans Indonesian and Asian — nasi goreng, tom yum seafood, kung pao chicken — with a bar built for sunset: the Tequila Sunrise is the house pour. Come straight off the sand; nobody dresses up here.
