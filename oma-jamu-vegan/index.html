About

Oma means grandma.
Jamu is the drink.

Oma Jamu started as a jamu vendor — the traditional Indonesian herbal drink of ginger and turmeric — and grew into a small vegan warung run by a local family. The name says what it is: grandma’s recipes, cooked at home, entirely from plants.

Everything is vegan, all day: the buffet, the breakfasts, the burgers and the bakery. No MSG, no palm oil. Dishes are labelled in English, with notes on what is gluten-free or spicy.

It is a quieter corner of Canggu, a few minutes’ ride up Batu Bolong street — worth the trip for a plate that costs less than a coffee back home.
