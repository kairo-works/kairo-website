Fire, smoke and a short menu

Ember started in 2019 with one wood oven, a short menu and a rule: if it does not go over fire, it does not go on the plate. The menu is written each morning around what the market brought in, and the same two people have run the kitchen and the room since the first night.

There are forty seats, a counter for walk-ins and a cellar of wines from small growers. Nothing here is rushed, and nothing is dressed up to look like more than it is.
