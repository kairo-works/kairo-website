Our story

Fresh fish, the Polynesian way

Moana brings the South Pacific to Batu Bolong: sashimi-grade yellowfin tuna from nearby waters, poke bowls, carpaccio and grilled fish — with sugarcane juices on the side. No frills, just the freshest fish, cooked simply.

It is a small, laid-back room under the palms, a short walk from the beach. Come for lunch, stay for golden hour.
