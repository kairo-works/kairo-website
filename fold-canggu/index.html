About

French pastry,
Bali mornings

Fold is an artisan bakery by Chef Maxime Chené, in the jade-green tiled house on Jalan Nelayan. French viennoiserie is at its core — croissants, canelés, sourdough — with breakfast plates, coffee and homemade gelato.

Come for a slow breakfast, a box of pastries to take home, or a coffee at the counter. Upstairs, the air-conditioned loft is a cool, quiet corner — and kids get the Lego corner. It is the original Canggu bakery, with sister spots in Ubud and Sanur.
