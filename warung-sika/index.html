The buffet

Pick your rice.
Point at the dishes.

The display window is the heart of Warung Sika: colourful dishes lined up behind glass, made fresh each morning. Choose white, red or yellow rice, then point at what you want with it — the dishes below are the ones guides and guests keep naming; the window itself decides the day’s list, and some dishes rotate.

About

Best food, fresh food,
good taste.

That is how Warung Sika describes itself — “best food, fresh food, good taste, friendly staff and owner” — and the guides agree: a family-run warung that locals and tourists both call one of the best in Canggu.

There is no menu to study and no fuss. Choose your rice, point at the colourful dishes in the display window, and sit down at a communal bamboo table. Rice paddies still show just out front, on one of Canggu’s favourite streets.

It is the cheapest honest lunch in Canggu: a full plate for about two dollars, open every day from nine in the morning until nine at night.
